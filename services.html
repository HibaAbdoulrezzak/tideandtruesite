FAQs

What’s the difference between a wedding planner, coordinator, and designer?
  • Planner: Manages the entire process from budgeting, vendor sourcing, and contracts to logistics and timelines.
  • Coordinator: Steps in closer to the wedding (usually 6–8 weeks out) to run the day smoothly.
  • Designer: Focuses purely on aesthetic and décor vision.
When should we book a wedding planner?

Ideally 12–18 months before the wedding. For coordination services, 6–8 weeks before the big day. The earlier you book, the more seamless and cost-effective the planning can be.

How much do wedding planning services cost?

Pricing depends on the level of service—full planning, partial planning, or month-of coordination. Each package is customized based on your guest count, complexity, and vision.

Will hiring a planner save us money?

Yes. A planner helps you allocate your budget strategically, negotiates with vendors, and prevents costly mistakes. Couples often find planners pay for themselves in stress saved and money managed.

Do you only work with certain vendors?

No—we can recommend trusted professionals we know deliver excellent results, but you’re free to choose your own vendors. Our role is to ensure everyone works together seamlessly.

Will you be there on the wedding day?

Absolutely. We’re the first ones in and the last ones out—handling logistics, vendor coordination, timeline management, and troubleshooting so you can stay present.

Can you help with cultural or religious weddings?

Yes, we specialize in tailoring weddings to each couple’s traditions, whether that’s cultural ceremonies, faith-based elements, or blended celebrations.

What happens if something goes wrong on the wedding day?

That’s exactly why we’re there. We troubleshoot behind the scenes—handling late vendors, décor adjustments, weather issues, or last-minute changes—so you never feel the stress.

Do you handle destination weddings?

Yes. We can manage logistics abroad, from sourcing venues to coordinating travel and guest accommodations.

How do we get started?

The first step is a complimentary consultation call. From there, we’ll recommend a planning package that fits your needs and vision.
