Our Story

Where artistry meets organization

We’re Maryam and Danielle—two Toronto-based wedding planners and creative minds who came together through a shared love of hospitality, event design, and the quiet rhythm of seamless wedding and event coordination. We founded Tide & True Weddings & Events to bring a refined yet welcoming approach to Toronto weddings, corporate events, and special celebrations. From luxury wedding planning to full-service event management, we help couples and clients create unforgettable moments with elegance and ease.

From intimate garden weddings to full-weekend wedding celebrations, our work balances bespoke wedding design with meticulous event logistics—so you can be fully present, grounded, and truly celebrated on your special day.

Our Process

1
Discovery

Design begins with listening

After you submit the Inquiry Form, we explore your vision, style, and guest experience goals. From intimate weddings to full-scale celebrations, we translate your ideas into a cohesive creative direction and custom wedding plan tailored to your unique day.

2
Direction

Editorial, elevated, effortless

Editorial, elevated, effortless Through moodboards, floorplans, tastings, and timelines, we craft a bespoke wedding design guided by clarity and a refined eye for timeless elegance and luxury wedding styling. Every detail is considered to ensure a seamless guest experience and event flow.

3
Execution

Elevated, unforgettable orchestration

We oversee full-service wedding production, vendor management, and day-of coordination, so you can be fully present. Expect seamless logistics, graceful pacing, and a beautifully orchestrated celebration—every element curated for a truly unforgettable wedding experience.
