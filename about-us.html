Our Story

Where artistry meets organization

We’re Maryam and Danielle—two creative minds who came together through a shared love of hospitality, design, and the quiet rhythm of seamless coordination. We founded Tide & True to bring a refined yet welcoming approach to weddings and events.

From intimate garden gatherings to full-weekend celebrations, our work balances aesthetic direction with meticulous logistics—so you can feel present, grounded, and truly celebrated.

Our Approach

For us, planning an event always starts with people. We take the time to get to know you—what excites you, what makes you feel at home, and what will make your celebration feel like yours.

From there, we bring together the creative and the practical: moodboards and floorplans, timelines and tastings. When the day arrives, you’ll be laughing, dancing, and making memories while we quietly take care of everything behind the scenes.
